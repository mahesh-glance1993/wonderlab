# 8-week social calendar (3 posts a week)

Starts **Monday 30 November 2026** and runs to **Sunday 24 January 2027**, covering exam revision, the Christmas and New Year break, and Sankranti. A "later in the year" section covers Bathukamma, Dasara, school reopening and Telangana Formation Day.

Telugu: **needs native-speaker check.**

**Rhythm:** Monday, Wednesday and Friday at about 7:30 pm IST (when parents check phones after dinner). Post every item to:
- YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels (same vertical video)
- WhatsApp Channel + your WhatsApp Status
- X: a one-line post with the link (optional)

Video numbers refer to `video-scripts.md`; message numbers refer to `whatsapp.md`. Dates of school exams and holidays differ between schools and change each year: check the Telangana school academic calendar and BSE Telangana announcements before posting dated claims.

Every post ends with: `learn.maheshglance.com · Free · English & తెలుగు`
