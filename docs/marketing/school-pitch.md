## 1. One-page pitch (print on A4)

### WonderLab: free learning app that follows the Telangana SCERT syllabus

**For:** Nursery to Class 10 · **Languages:** English and Telugu · **Cost of the pilot:** free

**What students get**
- Short lessons for every subject in the Telangana SCERT syllabus, Classes 1–10: Telugu, Hindi (from Class 6), English, Maths, EVS, Science / Physical Science / Biological Science, Social Studies.
- Worked examples, then practice questions with up to three hints. Mistakes are explained kindly, never shamed.
- **"What if?" explorers** for formulas: move a slider and see the answer change, with the reason. Example: Class 10 "What if? Move the object in front of a convex lens", Class 7 "What if? Simple interest", Class 6 "What if? Time difference between two places".
- Notes for every chapter: type or handwrite, saved per subject.
- Rhymes, stories, games and activities for Nursery–UKG.
- Works offline once lessons are downloaded.

**What the school gets**
- A free **4-week pilot for one class**.
- A simple usage summary at the end: lessons opened, questions practised, topics mastered.
- No cost, no hardware to buy: works on Android phones and tablets, iPhones/iPads (via Safari) and any computer browser.

**Safety and privacy**
- No ads. No chat, no public profiles, no leaderboards.
- Children are added with a first name or nickname only. No date of birth, no phone number.
- The school can ask us to delete the pilot data at any time.

**Please note:** WonderLab follows the SCERT chapter lists but writes its own explanations; it is not an official SCERT product. Telugu text is still being checked by native-speaker teachers, and we welcome corrections.

**Contact:** [Your name] · [WhatsApp number] · https://learn.maheshglance.com/schools

## 2. Ten-minute demo script (for principal + 1–2 teachers)

**Before you go:** charge your phone, carry a second phone or a tablet, download Class 10 Physical Science, Class 6 Science and Nursery rhymes for offline use, sign in to a demo account with a made-up learner (for example "Meera, Class 10"). Turn on airplane mode before the demo, to show offline use.

| Minute | What you do | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 | Hand over the one-page pitch. | "Thank you for 10 minutes. WonderLab is a free app that follows the Telangana SCERT syllabus, Class 1 to 10, in English and Telugu. I'll show you three things, then you can try it." |
| 1–3 | Open Lessons > Class 10 > Physical Science > *Refraction of Light at Curved Surfaces* > *Lens formula and magnification*. Scroll through the lesson blocks to the worked example. | "Each chapter is split into small lessons. Short explanation, pictures, a worked example with steps." |
| 3–5 | Open **"What if? Move the object in front of a convex lens"**. Hand the phone to the teacher and ask them to drag the slider. | "Students can move a value and see what changes, and why. This is the part teachers tell us is hard to show on a blackboard." |
| 5–6 | Open practice. Answer one question wrong on purpose. Show the kind feedback and a hint. | "A wrong answer gets an explanation, not a red cross. Up to three hints. A right answer gets 'Well done!'" |
| 6–7 | Tap the globe button to switch to Telugu. | "Every screen switches to Telugu. Our Telugu is being checked by teachers; if you see a mistake, please tell us." |
| 7–8 | Show Notes: tap Notes, type a line, then write by hand. | "Students keep their own notes for each chapter, typed or handwritten." |
| 8–9 | Show airplane mode is on. Switch to the Nursery–UKG home > Rhymes & Songs > "Chandamama, Come to Me". | "All of this is working offline right now. For the little ones we have rhymes, stories and games." |
| 9–10 | Close. | "We'd like to offer one class a free 4-week pilot. Which class would help you most? Who would be the teacher in charge?" Write down the name, class and a start date. |

**If they ask something you don't know:** "Good question. I'll check and message you today." Then do.

## 3. FAQ

**How much does it cost?**
The pilot is free for one class for 4 weeks. The app is free for families. If the school wants it for more classes next year, we will discuss a simple school price then; there is no obligation.

**What data do you collect about students?**
One class account. Each child is added with a first name or nickname only. No date of birth, phone number, address or photo. We store the child's progress (lessons, answers) so they can continue where they stopped. No ads and no third-party tracking. We delete the pilot data whenever the school asks.

**Can children chat with strangers?**
No. There is no chat, no public profile, no gallery and no leaderboard.

**Does it need internet?**
Only to sign in and download lessons. After that, downloaded lessons work offline; progress syncs when the device is online again.

**Which devices?**
Android phones and tablets (app from our website), iPhone and iPad (open in Safari and choose "Add to Home Screen"), and any computer browser. It also works on a projector from a laptop.

**Which syllabus and subjects?**
It follows the Telangana SCERT syllabus for Classes 1–10: Telugu, Hindi (from Class 6), English, Maths, EVS (Classes 1–5), Science (6–7), Physical Science and Biological Science (8–10) and Social Studies. Nursery–UKG has rhymes, stories, games and early activities.

**Is it approved by SCERT or the government?**
No. WonderLab follows the SCERT chapter lists but writes its own explanations. It is not an official SCERT product.

**Is the content checked by teachers?**
A teacher review is in progress. We welcome corrections from your teachers; there is a "Report a problem" button in every lesson.

**Is it in Telugu medium?**
Every screen can be switched to Telugu. Telugu wording is being checked by native-speaker teachers.
