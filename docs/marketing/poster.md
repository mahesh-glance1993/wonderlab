# A4 poster / pamphlet

Text for a one-page A4 poster or handout. A printable version is in `poster.html`: open it in Chrome, press Ctrl+P, choose A4, margins "Default", and turn on "Background graphics". Black-and-white printing works too.

Telugu: **needs native-speaker check.**

**QR code:** make a QR code for `https://learn.maheshglance.com` (Chrome: open the site, click the share icon in the address bar > "Create QR code" > Download). Print it and paste it in the dashed box, or open `poster.html` in a text editor and replace the box with `<img src="qr.png">` saved next to it.
