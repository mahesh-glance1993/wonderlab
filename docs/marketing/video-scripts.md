# YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels scripts

Twelve scripts, each 45 seconds or less. Every lesson and explorer named here exists in the app today; open it and check before you record.

Telugu voice-over: **needs native-speaker check.**

## How to record (free)

- **Screen:** Android: the built-in screen recorder (Quick Settings > Screen record). iPhone: Control Centre > Screen Recording. Laptop: the website in Chrome, recorded with OBS Studio (free) or Windows Snipping Tool's video mode.
- Record vertical (9:16). On a laptop, use Chrome DevTools device mode at 390 × 844 so the site looks like a phone.
- **Voice:** record on your phone in a quiet room, phone 15 cm from your mouth, under a blanket if the room echoes.
- **Edit:** YouTube Shorts editor, Instagram Reels editor, or CapCut (free). Add the English subtitle text as on-screen captions.
- **Never show a child's face, name or voice** without written permission from a parent (see `social-accounts.md`). Use a demo learner with a made-up nickname such as "Chinni" or "Arjun".
- Turn the app language to Telugu for the Telugu shots (globe button), and to English for subtitles' matching screens where useful.
- First frame = the hook text on screen in big letters. People decide in 1 second.
- End card (last 3 seconds): app icon + "learn.maheshglance.com" + "Free · English & Telugu".
- **YouTube audience setting:** each script says "Made for kids" or "Not made for kids". Choose that when you upload.

Hashtags to reuse: `#Telangana #TSSSC #SSC2027 #TeluguMedium #Class10 #LearnWithWonderLab #Hyderabad #FreeEducation`
