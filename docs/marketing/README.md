# WonderLab free marketing kit

This kit is for launching WonderLab with **no budget**. Everything here uses free channels: WhatsApp, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, school and tuition-centre visits, printed posters (a few rupees at a local xerox shop), and free tools to measure progress.

**Website:** https://learn.maheshglance.com · Android app: `/get-app` · iPhone/iPad: open the site in Safari, then Share > Add to Home Screen · Schools: `/schools` (free one-class pilot) · Help: `/support`

## What we can say, and what we must not say

Say this (it is true today):

- Free learning app and website for children, Nursery to Class 10.
- **Follows the Telangana SCERT syllabus** for Classes 1–10: Telugu, Hindi (from Class 6), English, Maths, EVS, Science / Physical Science / Biological Science, Social Studies.
- English and Telugu.
- Short lessons with pictures, worked examples, practice questions with hints and kind feedback.
- **"What if?" explorers**: move a slider, see the answer change and read why (for example "What if? Move the object in front of a convex lens" in Class 10 Physical Science).
- Notes: type or handwrite notes for each chapter and keep them.
- Rhymes, stories, games and activities for little ones (Nursery–UKG).
- Works offline once lessons are downloaded.
- No ads. No chat, no public profiles, no leaderboards. Children use a nickname, not their full name.

Never say this:

- "AI tutor", "AI teacher", "personal AI". The app does not chat with children.
- "Teacher-approved" or "verified by teachers", until the review in `docs/TEACHER_REVIEW.md` is finished.
- "SCERT approved", "official", "endorsed by the government", or using the SCERT/Telangana government logo. Say "follows the Telangana SCERT syllabus".
- "Guaranteed marks", "100% pass", "toppers use it". No promises about exam results.
- Exact counts ("10,000 questions") unless you have checked the number in the code that week.

Before you post anything with a child in it, read the rules in `social-accounts.md`: no child's face or full name without **written** permission from a parent.

## The 90-day launch plan

Start date: the Monday you begin (for example 23 November 2026). Days 1–90 take you to late February, which is the run-up to the Class 10 SSC exams (usually March; check the BSE Telangana timetable). Spend about 1 hour a day; school visits take a half-day each.

### Phase 1: Soft launch with 20–50 families (Weeks 1–3)

The goal is not big numbers. The goal is to find what breaks and what children actually enjoy, before strangers see it.

**Week 1: Get ready**
- Create the brand accounts (`social-accounts.md`): one Gmail, YouTube, Instagram, X. Turn on 2-step verification.
- Make a free WhatsApp Business account on a separate number if you can (a spare SIM). Set the business profile: name "WonderLab", website link, short description, and a greeting message. Create a **WhatsApp Channel** called "WonderLab updates".
- Make a Google Form "WonderLab feedback" (questions below) and a Google Sheet "WonderLab tracker" (columns below).
- Make a short link and a QR code for the website (free: the QR generator in Chrome's share menu, or any free QR site). Use the same QR everywhere.
- Test the full journey on 3 phones: one cheap Android, one mid-range Android, one iPhone. Install from `/get-app`, sign up, add a child, open a lesson, switch to Telugu, go offline, write a note.
- Print 20 copies of `poster.html` (A4, black and white is fine).

**Week 2: Invite the first 20 families**
- Personal message (not a broadcast) to 20 families you know with children in different classes: aim for a mix of Nursery–Class 2, Class 3–5, Class 6–8 and Class 10. Use WhatsApp message 1 in `whatsapp.md`.
- Offer to sit with 5 of them for 15 minutes while the child uses it. Watch silently. Write down where the child gets stuck.
- Ask each family to try it for **one week**, at least 3 times.

**Week 3: Fix and widen to 50**
- Fix the top 3 problems people hit (login confusion, slow download, a confusing screen).
- Send the feedback form to the first 20 families.
- Ask the happiest 5 families: "Can you forward this to one other parent?" (WhatsApp message 2). Grow to about 50 families.
- Post the first 3 YouTube Shorts / Reels (`video-scripts.md`, scripts 1, 7 and 10).

### Phase 2: Go public on free channels (Weeks 4–7)

**Week 4: WhatsApp and video rhythm**
- Start the 3-posts-a-week rhythm in `social-calendar.md`.
- Post in parent groups you are already a member of (WhatsApp message 2). Never join groups only to spam; one post per group, then answer questions.
- Share every new Short on the WhatsApp Channel and your WhatsApp Status.

**Week 5: Teachers and tuition centres**
- List 10 tuition centres and 10 private/government schools within reach (Google Maps, ask friends). Keep them in the tracker sheet.
- Send WhatsApp message 3 (teachers) to every teacher you know personally.
- Visit 3 tuition centres with printed posters (WhatsApp message 5 is the follow-up).

**Week 6: School visits**
- Visit 3–5 schools. Ask for the principal or the computer/science teacher. Use `school-pitch.md`: leave the one-page pitch, offer the **free one-class pilot**, book a 10-minute demo.
- Send WhatsApp message 4 the same evening to every principal you met.

**Week 7: First pilots**
- Run the 10-minute demo in each school that says yes.
- Set up the pilot class: one class account, first names or nicknames only. Note: an account holds at most 8 learner profiles today; see the internal note at the top of `school-pitch.md` before promising a whole class.
- Agree a 4-week pilot with one teacher. Ask them to give 10 minutes of class time twice a week.

### Phase 3: Exam season and proof (Weeks 8–13)

Class 10 SSC pre-final exams usually run January–February and the board exams in March. This is when Class 10 parents and students look hardest for help.

**Week 8 (around Sankranti):** festival greeting (WhatsApp message 8). Light week: post a Sankranti Short for little ones (kites, rhymes) and rest.

**Week 9: Class 10 push**
- Send WhatsApp message 6 (Class 10 exam season) to Class 10 parents and to tuition centres.
- Post the Class 10 "what if?" Shorts (scripts 1–4) again with new hooks.
- Offer tuition centres a free "formula afternoon": you show Class 10 students the explorers on a projector or phone for 30 minutes.

**Week 10: Collect proof**
- Ask pilot teachers for one sentence of feedback (with permission to quote it with their first name and school type, for example "Science teacher, Hanamkonda"). This is a quote, not "teacher-approved".
- Ask parents for short testimonials with permission. No child's face or full name.

**Week 11: Check the numbers and decide**
- Review the tracker (below). Which channel brought the most installs? Do more of that in weeks 12–13; stop the weakest one.

**Week 12: Pilot review meetings**
- Meet each pilot school. Show them their class's usage (lessons done, practice answered). Ask: "Would you like this for more classes next year?" Note who says yes. That is your first revenue lead.

**Week 13: Plan the next 90 days**
- Write down: total installs, weekly active learners, top 10 lessons, school yes/no, the 5 most common complaints.
- Decide which paid steps (below) are now worth it.

## How to collect feedback (free)

1. **Google Form** (free) with 6 questions, in English and Telugu:
   - Which class is your child in?
   - How many days did your child use WonderLab this week? (0 / 1–2 / 3–4 / 5+)
   - What did your child like most? (lessons / "what if?" sliders / practice / games / rhymes / notes / other)
   - What was confusing or did not work?
   - Would you tell another parent about WonderLab? (0–10)
   - Can we contact you for a 10-minute chat? (yes/no, phone optional)
   Do not ask for the child's name, school name of the child, or date of birth.
2. **In-app:** the "Report a problem" button (flag icon) in lessons sends reports to the app's own database. Read them every week.
3. **Sit-and-watch sessions:** 15 minutes, watch a child use it, say nothing, write down where they hesitate. Five of these teach more than 100 form replies.
4. **WhatsApp replies:** star useful messages and copy them into the tracker sheet every Friday.

## Simple metrics (free tools only)

Track 4 numbers every Monday in the Google Sheet "WonderLab tracker".

| Metric | What it means | Free tool |
|---|---|---|
| Installs / sign-ups | New parent accounts this week | The app's own Cloudflare D1 database (query below). Android APK downloads: Cloudflare dashboard > Analytics for the `/get-app` path |
| Weekly active learners | Child profiles that answered at least one question in the last 7 days | D1 query below |
| Lessons completed | Lesson progress records and objectives mastered this week | D1 query below |
| School enquiries | Forms from `/schools` + WhatsApp enquiries | Count by hand in the sheet |

Also note: video views per Short (YouTube Studio and Instagram Insights are free), WhatsApp Channel followers, and where each new family heard about you (ask in the first message: "How did you hear about WonderLab?").

**Website visits:** Cloudflare Web Analytics is free and uses no cookies. Turning it on needs a small site change (allowing its script in the Content-Security-Policy in `public/_headers`), so plan it as a separate engineering task.

**D1 queries** (run from the repo; replace `<7 days ago>` with a Unix time in seconds, for example from `date -d '7 days ago' +%s`). These return counts only, never names:

```bash
# new parent accounts in the last 7 days
npx wrangler d1 execute wonderlab --env production --remote --command \
  "SELECT COUNT(*) AS new_accounts FROM accounts WHERE role='parent' AND created_at >= <7 days ago>"

# weekly active learners (answered at least one question)
npx wrangler d1 execute wonderlab --env production --remote --command \
  "SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT profile_id) AS active_learners FROM attempts WHERE created_at >= <7 days ago>"

# lessons with progress saved this week, and objectives newly mastered
npx wrangler d1 execute wonderlab --env production --remote --command \
  "SELECT (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM lesson_state WHERE updated_at >= <7 days ago>) AS lessons_progressed, (SELECT COUNT(*) FROM mastery WHERE mastered_at >= <7 days ago>) AS objectives_mastered"

# learners by class (to see which classes you are reaching)
npx wrangler d1 execute wonderlab --env production --remote --command \
  "SELECT class_level, COUNT(*) AS learners FROM profiles GROUP BY class_level ORDER BY class_level"
```

All times in these tables are Unix seconds. Copy only the totals into the sheet.

**Targets for day 90 (realistic for one person, no budget):** 300–500 parent accounts, 100+ weekly active learners, 3–5 school pilots, 1–2 schools saying "yes" to next year. If you reach half of this, the plan is still working.

## When and how to move to paid options

Only spend money when it removes a real blocker or when revenue covers it.

| Paid step | Cost | Do it when |
|---|---|---|
| Google Play developer account | US$25, one time | 200+ Android installs from the APK, or a school says "we only install from Play Store". Use `store-listing.md`. Play needs a privacy policy URL and a Data safety form. Apps for children must also follow Google Play's Families policy. |
| Apple Developer Program | US$99 per year | Paying schools or many parents ask for an App Store app. Until then, "Add to Home Screen" in Safari works. |
| Razorpay (payments) | No setup fee; a small % per payment | You have a product to sell (school licence or revision pack) and need invoices and card/UPI/netbanking. Needs business KYC. |
| UPI "support WonderLab" | Free | Any time. A simple UPI QR on `/support` ("Like WonderLab? Support it with any amount"). Never make it look like children must pay. |
| Printing more posters / standees | Few hundred rupees | When a school or tuition centre agrees to display them. |

## Revenue path

1. **Free school pilots (now):** one class, 4 weeks, free. Prove that children use it.
2. **Paid school licences (next academic year, from June 2027):** a yearly per-school or per-class price that a budget private school can pay. Keep it simple: one price, all subjects, all classes. Before you send any price or agreement to a school, get the wording checked by someone who understands contracts, and check what India's data-protection law (the DPDP Act) needs for schools and children's data.
3. **Support / UPI (any time):** voluntary support from parents who like it. Keep learning free.
4. **Class 10 revision pack (later, after teacher review):** printable formula sheets, chapter-wise practice sets and quick revision notes, for a small one-time price. Only launch it after the content is checked by teachers (`docs/TEACHER_REVIEW.md`).

The core app stays free for families. Money comes from schools and optional extras.
