Use this when you pay for a Google Play developer account (US$25, one time) or the Apple Developer Program (US$99 a year). See the "When and how to move to paid options" section in `README.md`.

## Before you submit (internal checklist)

- Privacy policy URL on the website (both stores require one).
- Google Play: fill in **Data safety** truthfully (account data: username, optional email; app activity: lesson progress; no ads, no data sold). Set **Target audience** honestly: if children under 13 are a target, the app must meet Google Play's **Families policy**.
- Apple: if you list in the **Kids** category, no third-party analytics or ads, and links out of the app need a parental gate.
- Do not use the SCERT or Telangana government name or logo in a way that suggests an official product. "Follows the Telangana SCERT syllabus" is fine; "Official SCERT app" is not.
- Do not claim "Teacher Approved" (that is a Google Play programme badge you cannot self-award) or "AI tutor".
- Screenshots: use demo learners with made-up nicknames. No real children.

## Google Play (English)

**App name** (max 30): `WonderLab: Learn Class 1-10` (27)

**Short description** (max 80):
`Free lessons for Nursery to Class 10. Telangana syllabus. English and Telugu.` (77)

**Full description** (max 4000):

WonderLab is a free learning app for children from Nursery to Class 10. It follows the Telangana SCERT syllabus for Classes 1–10, and every screen works in English and Telugu.

LEARN EVERY SUBJECT
• Telugu, Hindi (from Class 6), English, Maths, EVS, Science, Physical Science, Biological Science and Social Studies
• Short lessons with pictures, key points and worked examples, chapter by chapter

PRACTISE WITH KIND FEEDBACK
• Practice questions with up to three hints
• Mistakes are explained, never shamed
• Encouragement when you get it right: "Well done! Keep it up!"

"WHAT IF?" EXPLORERS
Move a slider and watch the answer change, with a short explanation of why. Try the lens formula, Ohm's law, simple interest, the discriminant of a quadratic, time difference between places and many more.

NOTES FOR EVERY CHAPTER
Type notes or write by hand. Save key points from a lesson with one tap. Find them later by subject.

FOR LITTLE ONES (NURSERY–UKG)
Rhymes and songs, animated stories, matching and sorting games, colours, shapes and patterns.

WORKS OFFLINE
Download lessons once, then learn without internet. Progress syncs when you are back online.

SAFE AND CALM
• No ads
• No chat, no public profiles, no leaderboards
• Children use a nickname; no date of birth needed
• Parent area protected by the parent's password
• Text size and reduce-motion settings

FOR SCHOOLS
Free one-class pilot. Visit learn.maheshglance.com/schools

WonderLab follows the chapter lists of the Telangana SCERT textbooks and writes its own explanations. It is not an official SCERT or government app.
