1 Medium Density & Speed
check Understood / అర్థమైంది

మాధ్యమం సాంద్రత మరియు కాంతి వేగం

Air is an optically rarer medium, while glass is optically denser. When light enters glass, its electromagnetic speed slows down from 300,000 km/s to approximately 200,000 km/s.

The Toy Car Analogy: Imagine a toy car driving at an angle from a polished marble floor (Air) onto a thick, fluffy rug (Glass).

The front tire that hits the rug first slows down immediately, causing the car to swerve TOWARDS the imaginary straight perpendicular line!

3 Emergent Ray & Dispersion
lock Clue 3: Tucked away

What happens when the light leaves the glass and re-enters the air? (Denser to Rarer).

Revealing hints never reduces your mastery points or practice score.

The Exit Journey: Denser → Rarer

When light exits back from glass into air, it speeds back up! Because it moves into a rarer medium, it bends AWAY from the normal. At both surfaces, the deviations add up, creating the overall bending angle of the prism.

బయటకు వచ్చేటప్పుడు (గాజు నుండి గాలికి) లంబానికి దూరంగా వంగుతుంది!
