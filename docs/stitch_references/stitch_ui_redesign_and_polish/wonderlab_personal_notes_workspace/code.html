Aarav's Core Concept Explanation

When a ray of white light travels through empty air, it moves at top speed! But as soon as it enters something thicker—like water in a tumbler or glass in a prism—it slows down.

Imagine a toy car rolling fast from smooth marble tiles onto a thick coir carpet at an angle. The front left wheel hits the carpet first and slows down right away, while the right wheel is still spinning fast on the smooth floor. That causes the whole car to swivel and turn! Light does the exact same thing when entering optical mediums.
