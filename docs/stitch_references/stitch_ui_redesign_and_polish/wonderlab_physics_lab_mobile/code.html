Snell's Law in Plain Words

Light speeds along at nearly 300,000 km/s in air, but when entering dense glass, it slows down!

Because high-energy Violet waves slow down more than long Red waves, violet bends more sharply. This separation of white light into its component colors is called dispersion (VIBGYOR).

Real-World Optics
Monsoon Skies over Telangana

Millions of floating raindrops act like tiny spherical prisms! Sunlight enters the droplets, refracts, bounces off the back, and splits into the vibrant arcs visible above Hyderabad and Warangal.
