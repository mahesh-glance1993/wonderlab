# Telangana State Syllabus (SCERT Telangana / BSE Telangana): Classes 1-10

Reference for WonderLab content writers. It lists the textbooks, chapters and the topics inside each chapter. **Write original explanations. Never copy textbook text.** The topic lists are short paraphrases of the books' own section headings.

- **Board:** Board of Secondary Education, Telangana (SSC, Class 10). Textbooks are prepared by SCERT Telangana and printed by the Telangana Govt Text Book Press.
- **Academic year:** 2025-26. The official site now carries only 2026-27 files, so every chapter list here was read from those 2026-27 PDFs.
- **Retrieved:** 2026-11-13. All books were downloaded from the official SCERT e-textbook listing (scert.telangana.gov.in, Publications > eTextBooks). That page links to SCERT-hosted PDFs and to Google Drive files. The Drive links come from the official page, so they count as *Official*.

- **2025-26 vs 2026-27:** The Class 10 books (and the other Class 6-10 books checked) carry the imprint "Republished 2025, 2026" with no revision notice. Their 2025-26 chapter lists are therefore the same as the 2026-27 PDFs used here. Any differences found in a particular book are recorded in that book's notes.
- **Important correction:** The current Class 10 Physical Science book has **no "Heat" chapter and no "Refraction of Light at Plane Surfaces" chapter**. Snell's law, total internal reflection, critical angle and specific heat are taught in **Class 9 Physical Science**. Class 10 begins with "Reflection of Light at Curved Surfaces". Some older guides and study sites still list the older 13- or 14-chapter order.
- **Medium:** Science, maths and social books are listed in their **English-medium** editions. The same SCERT page also offers Telugu-medium books, and bilingual Part 1/Part 2 books (Telugu + English on facing pages) for the government-school bilingual medium. It also carries Urdu, Hindi, Kannada, Marathi and Tamil medium books. Some of those other-medium files are still the 2020-21 editions. The chapter content is the same across media.
- **Language papers:** Telugu is the First Language. Hindi is the Second Language from Class 6. English is the Third Language in the SSC scheme. Students in other media take their mother tongue (for example Urdu) as First Language, and Telugu or Hindi as the Second Language. "Other Media" Telugu books exist for non-Telugu-medium students. Composite courses (Telugu + Sanskrit, Urdu + Arabic) also exist, and SCERT publishes Sanskrit composite (CC) and oriental (OC) books.
- **Extra Class 9-10 book:** *Environmental Education* is a book of project-style lessons. It is assessed internally and is not a theory paper.

- **Sources for the pattern:** The 80 + 20 split and the 28/80 written pass mark come from G.O.Ms.No.2, School Education, dated 26.08.2014 (Official): <https://bse.telangana.gov.in/images/Gov_GO.pdf>. The rule that the 80:20 pattern continues for 2025-26 comes from Deccan Chronicle, 12 Aug 2025 (Secondary): <https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/telangana/telangana-retains-ssc-assessment-pattern-1896920>. A 2024 proposal to drop internal marks was put on hold.
- **Pass mark:** 35% per subject, with at least 28/80 in the written paper. The 2014 G.O. set two 40-mark papers per subject. Since 2023 the board has held one paper per subject, with Science split into Physical Science and Biological Science parts of 40 marks each. The parts have sometimes been written on separate days. That detail comes from news coverage (Secondary). Check it against the current BSE Telangana time table and model papers.
- **Question-paper blueprint:** The section-wise blueprint (for example, how many 1-, 2-, 4- and 6-mark questions) for March 2026 was not found on bse.telangana.gov.in. Study sites give conflicting versions, so it is **Unverified** here.
- **English summative domains** (official, from the Class 10 English textbook, Appendix 2): reading comprehension, vocabulary and grammar, and creative expression (major and minor discourses such as letter, diary, notice, conversation, story, report, profile and review).

**Complete chapter lists (Official) for every book, Classes 1-10**

- Hindi starts in **Class 6** for Telugu- and English-medium students.

**Known gaps and uncertainties**

- **Telugu grammar.** These books use legacy font encoding, so their pages were read as images. Grammar topics are listed per lesson for Class 10 and for Class 9 lessons 1, 3, 5, 7 and 8 only. For Classes 1-8 and the other Class 9 lessons, only the lesson type and theme are recorded.
- **Hindi grammar ("bhasha ki baat").** Recorded per lesson for Classes 9-10. Not recorded for Classes 6-8.
- **English grammar per unit.** Complete for Classes 7-10. Partial for Classes 4-6. For Classes 1-3, only themes and in-unit headings are recorded. Class 3-5 contents pages do not mark which readings are poems.
- **Lower-class maths topics.** Class 1-2 maths topics are inferred from chapter titles only. Class 3-5 topics are paraphrased from activity headings, because those books have no numbered sections.
- **Partly legible items.** In Class 1 Telugu, lesson 23's title, and the letter glyphs for lessons 21-23, were hard to read from the scan; verify them against the PDF. In Class 2 English, the unit 4 title is only partly legible.
- **Possible errors or inferences.** Class 7 English unit 7: the contents page and the unit's self-assessment name different "A" readings; the contents page is followed. Class 8 Physical Science chapter numbers were inferred from chapter-opening pages, because the index lists titles out of order.
- **2025-26 versus 2026-27.** All books used are the 2026-27 PDFs on the official site. The imprints of the Class 6-10 books say "Republished 2025, 2026", so their chapter lists should match 2025-26 (Class 7 Telugu says only "Republished 2025"). New in 2026-27: an "AI and Digital Literacy" / "Digital Literacy" chapter in the Maths books for Classes 1-5. These chapters may not have been in the 2025-26 books.
- **Not covered.** Urdu, Hindi, Kannada, Marathi and Tamil medium books (same content, other languages); Urdu FL; Sanskrit composite and oriental books; "Other Media" Telugu books (URLs are noted in the Class 9 and 10 entries); the Environmental Education books (Class 10 list is in the notes only).
- **Non-numeric chapter numbers.** Some supplementary or non-detailed lessons carry string values in the `no` field (for example "U1", "*", "11A"). Code that reads the JSON must accept strings there.
